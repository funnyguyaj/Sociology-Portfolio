# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A personal portfolio for Analicia Parish, a sociology graduate. Three audiences, roughly in this order:

- **Employers and hiring managers** in research, nonprofit, policy, communications and advertising roles. They are judging fit and credibility, usually after following a link from a résumé, LinkedIn profile or application.
- **Graduate school committees** (sociology MA/PhD) reading up on her research interests, writing and academic background.
- **Editors and collaborators** who might commission writing or partner on projects.

All three arrive with a specific question ("who is this, what has she done, is she serious?"), scan quickly, then read the work that interests them.

## Product Purpose

A single home base that presents Analicia as a sociologist and writer. It should show her academic credentials, her published research and journalism, and her experience, and give interested visitors a clear way to get in touch.

The site succeeds when a reader can see within seconds what her background is and what she writes about, open her actual work, and find out how to reach her.

## Positioning

The work is the differentiator. She has a published honors thesis on race, gender and punk resistance, and opinion journalism on gender expression, queer media and period equity. Together they give her a clear, specific body of work on identity, gender, race and culture. The site should let that work lead instead of generic "passionate about people" claims.

## Operating Context

- Visitors mostly arrive through a shared link (résumé, LinkedIn, application, email) on desktop or phone.
- Reading sessions are short and evaluative; publications open on external sites (USF Digital Collections, the San Francisco Foghorn).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: plain HTML and CSS (`index.html`, `style.css`), no framework or build step.
- **Structure: one page** with these sections: intro, About (bio), Publications, Experience, Contact, plus a portrait photo.
- Publications are a list of external links, each with a year.
- Undecided: hosting/deploy target; whether a downloadable PDF CV is offered; contact method (email link vs form).

## Product Principles

1. **The work leads.** Real publications and credentials carry the page. Self-description stays brief and specific.
2. **Credible at a glance.** Every audience is evaluating, so name, field, institution and the body of work should be legible immediately.
3. **Nothing invented.** Missing content shows as an obvious placeholder, never filler that looks real.
4. **One page, easy to scan.** Visitors arrive from links and leave quickly, so sections stay short and the path to the work and to contact is direct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement was given. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline, since academic and institutional reviewers may use assistive tech.
