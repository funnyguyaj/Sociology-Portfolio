ANALICIA

A Curiosity for People.

I'm Analicia, a Sociology graduate from the University of San Francisco, where I pursued an Honors Concentration in Sociology alongside a minor in Advertising. My academic journey has been driven by a curiosity about people: how we think, how we connect, and how the communities and institutions around us shape our experiences. What draws me to sociology is its ability to turn everyday observations into deeper questions about the world. Through research, writing, and community engagement, I've developed an appreciation for the stories, perspectives, and experiences that make every community unique. I hope to bring that curiosity into everything I do, using thoughtful research, creative communication, and a willingness to see the world from different perspectives to contribute to meaningful change.
